summary: "The Power & Politics segment discussed the federal government's tabling of Bill C-41, which indefinitely maintains the exclusion of medical assistance in dying (MAID) for Canadians whose sole underlying condition is mental illness while amending the Criminal Code to permit advance requests for MAID. The bill allows adults diagnosed with incurable conditions like Alzheimer's or Parkinson's to file non-expiring requests that can be withdrawn, subject to rigorous safeguards such as two independent practitioners' agreement, confirmation of intolerable suffering at the time of provision, and a 30-day pause if resistance is shown. It requires provincial legislative regimes for implementation, potentially leading to variation in access.\n\nThe interview featured an extended conversation with Justice Minister Sean Fraser, who cited the joint parliamentary committee's recommendation, lack of consensus on the irremediable nature of mental illness, and inadequate community mental health services. No other guests appeared; the host pressed on European models, prior government commitments, expected legal challenges, and risks of a provincial patchwork.

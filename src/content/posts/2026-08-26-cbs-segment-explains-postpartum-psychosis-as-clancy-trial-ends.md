Clinical psychologist and neuroscientist Heather Berlin joined the anchor to differentiate postpartum depression (a common mood disorder) from postpartum psychosis (a rare thought disorder involving a break from reality). \n\nBerlin explained there is no simple test for postpartum psychosis, stressed the need for correct diagnosis because SSRIs for depression can worsen psychosis or induce mania in those with bipolar disorder, and called for reduced stigma, better screening, family awareness, and improved treatment access. The discussion ended by noting that even with psychosis, legal questions of knowing right from wrong and controlling behavior remain central to the case. Sources were primarily the trial proceedings, Cleveland Clinic data, and Berlin's clinical expertise."

summary: "The CBC segment reports on draft legislation buried in the 521-page Budget 2026 implementation bill that would grant the federal Crown corporation Alto bespoke expropriation powers for the planned 1,000-km high-speed rail line between Toronto and Quebec City. Key changes include eliminating independent public hearings (replaced by written objections), allowing expropriated land to vest directly in private consortium partners rather than only the Crown, permitting the government to register prohibitions on work or sales for up to eight years (with four-year bans on improvements), and routing disputes over grade-separated crossings to private arbitration where landowners may share costs up to $50,000. Transport Minister Steven MacKinnon defended the measures as providing flexibility, noting similar regimes used for Ontario Line and Montreal REM projects, and emphasized the narrow 60-metre corridor footprint. Alto issued a statement affirming streamlined processes retain owner protections and that corridor studies do not guarantee acquisition."

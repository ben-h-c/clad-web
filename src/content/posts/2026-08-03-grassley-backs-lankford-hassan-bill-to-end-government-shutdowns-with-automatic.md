He reviewed the standard appropriations process, explained that failure to pass 12 bills or a CR by Sept. 30 leads to shutdowns, and criticized the increasing use of shutdown threats for partisan leverage. Grassley noted the costs, service disruptions, and erosion of trust from shutdowns, arguing they are ineffective bargaining tools. The bill would provide automatic continuing resolutions on short-term rolling bases at prior-year funding levels and impose restrictions on congressional travel, recesses, and non-appropriations legislation to force completion of work. He called for ending 'Groundhog Day' crisis governance."

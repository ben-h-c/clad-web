Harris noted Texas produces about 60 million barrels of produced water daily, most of which is reinjected, and highlighted the legislature-created Produced Water Consortium partnering with Natura. When asked about Congress's role, Harris suggested rolling back outdated federal regulations but otherwise letting Texas proceed independently. The session shifted to Rep.

It includes a counter from Distilled Spirits Council CEO Chris Swonger denying any 'switch,' government control, or data sharing, calling such claims scare tactics. Opponents also cite risks of false positives, such as mistaking evasive swerving for impairment. The bill awaits a hearing after being introduced last year."

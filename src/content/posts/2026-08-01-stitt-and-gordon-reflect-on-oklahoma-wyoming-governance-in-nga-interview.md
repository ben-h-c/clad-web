summary: "In this National Governors Association session at the Cowboy Hall of Fame in Oklahoma, outgoing Governor Asa Hutchinson interviewed Governors Kevin Stitt (Oklahoma) and Mark Gordon (Wyoming) on their paths to office, how their views of service evolved, highlights of their eight-year tenures, and advice for young people. Stitt described entering politics as a businessman frustrated by teacher walkouts, budget deficits, and Oklahoma's last-place incarceration ranking, leading to major criminal justice reforms. Gordon recounted a more unexpected entry from ranching and treasurer roles, focusing on economic diversification away from coal dependence. Both discussed the limits of gubernatorial power, the need for persuasion and cross-aisle work, inspiring youth, and post-office plans centered on family and continued service. The conversation repeatedly praised federalism, state-level innovation, and practical problem-solving over national polarization."

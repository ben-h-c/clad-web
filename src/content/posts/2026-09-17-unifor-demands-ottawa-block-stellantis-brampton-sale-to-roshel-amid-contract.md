summary: "The Power & Politics segment examines Stellantis' memorandum of understanding to sell its long-idled Brampton, Ontario assembly plant to Canadian armored-vehicle manufacturer Roshel. Host David Cochrane interviews Unifor National President Lana Payne, who wrote an open letter to Industry Minister Mélanie Joly seeking clarity on the government's role and demanding action to preserve vehicle assembly rather than accept the sale. The plant halted production in late 2023 for retooling to build Jeep Compass vehicles; that project was paused in early 2025 and the model relocated to the United States, leaving more than 2,200 workers on indefinite layoff.\n\nPayne argues the sale would mark the first auto-plant loss in the current trade war, violate prior commitments tied to federal funding, and fail to transfer strong auto-sector wages and benefits. She notes bargaining with Stellantis is at an impasse ahead of the collective agreement's expiry on Sept. 20, 2026, though a strike is a last resort. Joly is quoted saying Ottawa is applying 'maximum pressure' for a new Stellantis model at the site and will protect workers in any sale. The interview focuses on union demands for time to find auto alternatives, successor rights questions, and the need for government transparency."
